Your recovery journey

Rehabilitation that progresses with you

Good rehabilitation combines assessment, clear goals, gradual loading, movement practice and regular review.

Whether returning to daily activities or preparing for sport, your programme should match your current ability and medical guidance.

Discuss Your Goals

What your plan may include

  • Initial assessment and goal setting
  • Pain-aware mobility and movement work
  • Progressive strength and control exercises
  • Balance, coordination and functional training
  • Sport-specific progression when appropriate
  • Home exercise guidance and progress reviews

Exercise selection and progression depend on assessment, diagnosis, healing stage and clinician advice.
